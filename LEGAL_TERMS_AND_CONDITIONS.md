### SECTION [X] — BUYER LEAGUE SCORING & POINTS ALLOCATION FORMULA

Points in the Buyer League shall be awarded strictly pursuant to the following telemetry-verified allocation protocol:

1. **Per-Vehicle Bidding Allocation (1 Point Per Unique Vehicle):**
   * A Dealership earns exactly **1 point** for submitting one or more verified bids on a single unique vehicle lot during an official Auction Session.
   * **Anti-Farming Rule:** Submitting multiple bids, incremental bids, or counter-bids on the exact same vehicle lot will yield zero additional points beyond the initial **1 point**. This regulation guarantees equal opportunity across catalog sizes and rewards genuine multi-inventory acquisition interest.
2. **Match-Day Attendance Allocation (1 Point Per Session):**
   * An authenticated Dealership that logs into the live auction platform and maintains active connectivity during a scheduled weekly Auction Session earns **1 attendance point** per match day.
   * Only 1 attendance point can be earned per Dealership per scheduled match-day, regardless of the number of terminal logins or simultaneous devices utilized by the Dealership.
3. **Full-Season Loyalty Attendance Bonus (+20 Bonus Points):**
   * Dealerships that achieve flawless, unbroken attendance across all **[X]** scheduled match-day Auction Sessions during the Tournament Period shall be credited with an aggregate loyalty bonus of **20 bonus points**.
   * Missing even [X] scheduled session for any reason (including technical failure, ISP disruptions, or commercial absence) disqualifies the Dealership from receiving this bonus allocation.

### SECTION [X] — SELLER LEAGUE SCORING & POINTS ALLOCATION FORMULA

Points in the Seller League shall be awarded strictly pursuant to the following verified transaction protocol:

1. **Vehicle Consignment & Catalog Publishing (5 Points Per Vehicle):**
   * A Dealership earns **5 points** for each vehicle consigned, inspected, certified, and successfully published into the active auction inventory for a scheduled Auction Session.
   * Re-listing a vehicle that was previously listed without completing an arm's-length transaction or withdrawing a listing prior to session conclusion will result in the forfeiture of the corresponding **5 points**.
2. **Transaction Commission Settlement (5 Points Per Settled Transaction):**
   * A Dealership earns **5 points** for each completed vehicle transaction wherein the auction settlement, transfer formalities, and applicable TVS Certified platform commission are reconciled and cleared within **[X] banking days** following auction termination.

### SECTION [X] — EQUAL-POINTS TIE-BREAKER PROTOCOL & PRIZE COMBINATION FORMULA

To eliminate arbitrary tie-breaking metrics, subjective appraisals, or non-commercial parameters, TVS Certified enforces a mandatory **Equal Points Combined Purse Split Policy**. 

Where two or more Dealerships conclude the Tournament Period with identical total points within a podium tier, the cash awards allocated to the tied positions will be pooled and distributed in equal shares among the tied Dealerships:

1. **Case [X] — Two-Way Tie for First Place:**
   * If two Dealerships tie for First Place, the First Place purse (**₹25,000**) and the Second Place purse (**₹15,000**) are combined into an aggregate pool of **₹40,000**.
   * Each tied Dealership receives an equal payout of **₹20,000** (Rupees Twenty Thousand Only).
   * The Dealership with the next highest point total is awarded Third Place and receives the full Third Place prize of **₹10,000**.
2. **Case [X] — Three-Way Tie for First Place:**
   * If three Dealerships tie for First Place, all three podium cash purses (**₹25,000** + **₹15,000** + **₹10,000** = **₹50,000**) are pooled into an aggregate sum.
   * Each tied Dealership receives an equal payout of **₹16,666.67** (Rupees Sixteen Thousand Six Hundred Sixty-Six and Sixty-Seven Paise Only).
3. **Case [X] — Two-Way Tie for Second Place:**
   * The undisputed First Place winner receives the full First Place award of **₹25,000**.
   * The Second Place purse (**₹15,000**) and the Third Place purse (**₹10,000**) are combined into an aggregate pool of **₹25,000**.
   * Each tied Dealership receives an equal payout of **₹12,500** (Rupees Twelve Thousand Five Hundred Only).
4. **Case [X] — Two-Way Tie for Third Place:**
   * The undisputed First Place winner receives **₹25,000** in full, and the undisputed Second Place winner receives **₹15,000** in full.
   * The Third Place purse (**₹10,000**) is divided equally between the two tied Dealerships, with each receiving **₹5,000** (Rupees Five Thousand Only).
5. **Physical Trophies & Plaques in Ties:**
   * In any tied scenario, TVS Certified shall commission duplicate official trophies or plaques so that each qualifying entity receives the appropriate physical ceremonial award without dilution.
6. **Digital Interface Display Caveat:**
   * Where UI displays require sequential listing, secondary display-only ordering criteria shall be: (a) Total volume of unique vehicles bid/listed, followed by (b) Earliest server timestamp of the first earned point. **However, financial disbursement shall strictly obey the mathematical split rules detailed above.**
